# Tutorial center contract

- Home has one compact tutorial entry immediately after its primary shortcut grid. Existing shortcuts and bottom navigation are unchanged.
- Fourteen static course entries with versioned movies. The original thirteen lessons retain their complete metadata and media. Merchant v3 begins at the homepage mall banner, then the mall's 店家設定 button, before store registration and product listing. It retains sharing and the online-point workflow preview. Its narration, captions and player note explicitly say that online points await safety acceptance and are not yet released; tutorial publishing does not enable commerce point transactions.
- Four exclusive categories cover every lesson exactly once: 會員與名片 (4), 店家與點數 (3), 活動與課程 (6), AI任務 (1). The home entry defaults to 會員與名片, not an unbounded all-course list. Four category labels stay on one horizontal row, never wrap into a grid or vertical stack. Normal phone/desktop widths show all four; unusually narrow widths or enlarged text scroll horizontally inside the category row without widening the page. Category buttons have a minimum 44px touch height and expose their counts to screen readers and pressed state; the selected category's count also remains visible in the summary. Only the selected category's lessons render. Category selection never loads media. Returning from a player retains the category, scroll position and lesson focus; Close/Escape still restores the original page opener. Contextual direct-video entries remain direct and unchanged.

Versioned 2026-10-10 master and chapters are measured after export.
